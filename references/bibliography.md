## Key Papers by Warren B. Powell

### Unified Framework
- Powell, W.B. (2019). "A unified framework for stochastic optimization." *European Journal of Operational Research*, 275(3), 795-821.
- Powell, W.B. (2020). "From Reinforcement Learning to Optimal Control: A Unified Framework for Sequential Decisions." *Handbook of RL and Control*, Springer.

### Cost Function Approximations
- Ghadimi, S., Perkins, R.T., & Powell, W.B. (2020). "Reinforcement Learning via Parametric Cost Function Approximation for Multistage Stochastic Programming." arXiv:2001.00831.
- Powell, W.B. (2022). "The Parametric Cost Function Approximation: A New Approach for Multistage Stochastic Programming." arXiv:2201.00258.
- Ghadimi, S. & Powell, W.B. (2024). "Stochastic search for a parametric cost function approximation: Energy storage with rolling forecasts." *European Journal of Operational Research*.

### Knowledge Gradient and Optimal Learning
- Frazier, P.I., Powell, W.B., & Dayanik, S. (2008). "A Knowledge-Gradient Policy for Sequential Information Collection." *SIAM J. Control and Optimization*, 47(5), 2410-2439.
- Frazier, P.I., Powell, W.B., & Dayanik, S. (2009). "The Knowledge-Gradient Policy for Correlated Normal Beliefs." *INFORMS J. Computing*, 21(4), 599-613.
- Powell, W.B. & Ryzhov, I.O. (2012). *Optimal Learning*. Wiley.

### Approximate Dynamic Programming
- Ryzhov, I.O., Mes, M.R.K., Powell, W.B., & van den Berg, G. (2019). "Bayesian Exploration for Approximate Dynamic Programming." *Operations Research*, 67(1).
- Han, B. & Powell, W.B. (2020). "Optimal Online Learning for Nonlinear Belief Models Using Discrete Priors." *Operations Research*.

### Energy Applications
- Powell, W.B. & Meisel, S. (2016). "Tutorial on Stochastic Optimization in Energy Part II: An Energy Storage Illustration." *IEEE Trans. Power Systems*, 31(2), 1468-1475.
- Deng, Y. & Santos, B.F. (2022). Application of CFA to aircraft maintenance scheduling.

### Stochastic Optimization Landscape
- Powell, W.B. "From the Jungle of Stochastic Optimization to Sequential Decision Analytics." https://castle.princeton.edu/jungle/
